The straight-strip model assumes uniform pure bending. For a single-stage crossed-flexure pivot (CFP), it gives a lower bound per straight clamped blade. If the lower bound is within the limit, check the peak strain over the full travel using ANSYS or a measurement. Enter the highest absolute strain from that check for a CFP or custom joint.
